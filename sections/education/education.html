Chavaya Religious School

Sunday learning for grades K–12, open to all. New students are always welcome.

Grades 3–7 also get one-on-one online Hebrew tutoring at a time that suits you; we've taught Hebrew online for over 15 years. Grades 8 and up meet twice a month.

We offer experiential Jewish learning: pride in being Jewish, comfort with t'filah (prayer), a personal understanding of God, and Jewish history through Torah and other sources, plus individualized weekly Hebrew tutoring focused on reading and prayer.

Our mission

  • Familiarity with and knowledge of Jewish traditions
  • Love and respect for Torah
  • Connection to the people and land of Israel
  • Understanding of Jewish history
  • Responsibility to the world through Derech Eretz (ethical decency) and Tikkun Olam (repairing the world)

Our core values

  • Kehilah Kedoshah: a holy community
  • Shalom Bayit: peace in the home
  • Kavod: respect and honor
  • Chesed: loving-kindness
