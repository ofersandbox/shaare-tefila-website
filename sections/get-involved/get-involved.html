Chavurot (friendship circles)

Formed through the Membership Committee, a typical group has about seven couples of similar age and location; most are empty nesters. Each group sets its own schedule and activities: discussions, theater outings, meals, even a tour of the DC Historic Synagogue. No officers, dues or minutes. Members often sit together at congregational events. As one member put it, it's "an extended family, being there in good times as well as bad."

Chesed (caring) Committee

  • Rides to Shabbat services, events, medical appointments or the pharmacy
  • Chug Chesed / Kindness Circle: a bimonthly support group for members whose family or friends have dementia
  • Kosher dinners, including vegetarian, for homebound members, one-time or ongoing
  • Visits and calls for anyone who would welcome them
  • Shiva meals after a funeral or on return from an out-of-town funeral

Social Action

Rooted in the teaching that the world rests on Torah, worship and acts of loving kindness. Get involved through the Chesed Committee and through social justice programs in Olney and the greater Washington area.

Sisterhood

Programs that support personal growth, Jewish involvement, community and friendship.

Scrip program: buy gift cards from participating merchants, including grocery stores, and support Sisterhood. Overview flyer · Scrip flyer

Men's Club

Social, physical and spiritual activities. We take part in Seaboard Region programs of the Federation of Jewish Men's Clubs: the annual sukkah build, High Holiday volunteering and the "It's Academic" knowledge competition.

Sports: two teams each in the spring and fall inter-synagogue softball leagues, and two basketball teams in a winter league.

Youth groups

Open to children in grades K–12 throughout the year.
