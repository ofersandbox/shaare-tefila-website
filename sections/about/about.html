Our story

Shaare Tefila is rich in history and in Jewish community involvement.

We have programs for young and old, all in a warm, personal, caring environment. Our strengths include educational programs for everyone, from very young children through adults. Now in our 75th year, we remain a place where you will always belong.

75 years

Founded in 1951. Our 2026–27 Diamond Jubilee year brings special anniversary events alongside monthly Shabbat dinners, Havdalah experiences and holiday programs.

We are a Keshet Safe Zone, welcoming LGBTQ+ Jews and their families.

Inclement weather policy

Safety comes first. Please don't attend if getting here would put you in danger.

How we tell you

We try to send an email, post on Facebook and Instagram, and update the voicemail at (301) 593-3410. The voicemail changes only when the building is closed, and it gives the closure dates. It isn't always possible to make these updates.

Meetings & events

For daytime, evening and Sunday meetings, the Executive Director decides together with the relevant committee chair or professional. Cancellations are announced by email, Facebook and Instagram.

Minyans

If Montgomery County Public Schools have a 2-hour delay, morning minyan stays in person. If MCPS is closed, it is Zoom only. Evening minyan is cancelled if the county cancels evening school activities.

Celebrate your simcha with us

Anniversary and retirement parties, b'nai and b'not mitzvah celebrations, weddings and other special occasions.

  • Flexible room layout and a fully equipped, modern kitchen for many kinds of catering
  • Table configurations for up to 250 guests
  • A large dance floor
  • Shaare Tefila observes the dietary laws and requires a certified kosher caterer
