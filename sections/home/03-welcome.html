Welcome

Shaare Tefila is a warm, dynamic community that celebrates the three pillars of Jewish life: Torah, worship, and acts of loving kindness.

We offer programs for every age, from our monthly preschool to adult Torah study, in a warm, personal, caring environment. Whether you're new to the area, new to Judaism or coming home, there's a place for you here.
